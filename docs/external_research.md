# External research log – the 10 applicants and two portfolio overlaps

2026-11-24. Method: 13 web searches in standard mode. **Each result is a search-engine summary of pages, not a page I read in full.** Several claims rest on a single outlet, on vendor marketing, or on a headline. Nothing here has been checked against primary documents (for example the RBI circulars themselves). Treat every item as a lead with a stated source quality, not as a verified fact.

Source quality labels: **Press** (news outlet), **Vendor** (company marketing or product page), **Aggregator** (data site), **Blog / practitioner** (law-firm or compliance blog, individual spec), **Academic / body** (institution page).

This log feeds the evidence records and criteria B3 (regulatory path), B6 (competition honesty) and disqualifier D1 (portfolio conflict) in `scoring_framework.md`. It scores nothing.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **RentRoute: a rule that bears directly on the business.** Press reports say the RBI updated its Payment Aggregator Master Directions on 15 Sep 2025, effective immediately: only merchants with a direct contract with the payment aggregator can receive credit-card payments, and most landlords are not such merchants. PhonePe, Paytm and CRED suspended card rent payments within days; Amazon Pay, Freecharge and Mobikwik had paused earlier. RentRoute was incorporated in May 2026 and reports 6.1 Cr of rent paid since July 2026 through a payment-aggregator partner. How it operates under the rule is not in the files and is an open question (section 3.6). The deck's "no direct competitors" claim is also contradicted by the history of large apps offering this service.
2. **Talkbook vs MeetRecord: function-level overlap is real, and the portfolio company may have been renamed.** Press reports describe MeetRecord as AI call summaries, CRM-linked insights and call scoring for sales teams, and one listing says it is now **Outdoo AI**. A conflict check that matches on the name "MeetRecord" alone could miss a renamed company.
3. **Talkbook's "no India-first player" claim is contradicted** by several Indian vendors found (section 3.9), though all accuracy claims are vendor-reported.
4. **StockSathi vs SuperK:** SuperK (portfolio company) says it aims to expand into Telangana, where StockSathi operates, so adjacency may become competition.
5. **CAPilot, Pumpwise, Yatrika, StockSathi and GrainKind each face named competitors that their decks omit or understate** (sections 3.1, 3.4, 3.5, 3.8, 3.10).
6. **Not researched at all:** founders (all profiles are placeholders), whether any applicant name matches a real company, call-recording law, Telangana clinic registration, the market-input claims in the decks, and trade-credit rules for unregistered lenders (section 5).

### 3.2 CareCorner (clinics with diagnostics)
- **Regulation:** the Clinical Establishments Act (2010) applies only in states that adopted it (one source: 12 states and 7 union territories); where it applies the definition covers clinics, dispensaries and similar. A 2026 Maharashtra bill would require registration of every clinical establishment including labs and diagnostic centres. Sources: [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/?p=138383), [PRS](https://prsindia.org/bills/states/the-maharashtra-clinical-establishments-registration-and-regulation-bill-2026). Quality: law-firm commentary and legislative brief.
- **Market:** organised diagnostics operators are expanding into smaller cities; micro-hospital and small-clinic models are described as emerging. Quality: press and law-firm commentary.
- **Gap:** whether and how Telangana applies the Act to a clinic with in-house diagnostics and a pharmacy was **not found**. Direct Hyderabad competitors were not searched.

### 3.3 EarnWise (earned wage access)
- **Rule:** the RBI's Digital Lending Directions, 2025 (8 May 2025) apply to regulated entities such as banks and NBFCs. A compliance blog says earned-wage-access products fall within the framework when structured as loans or NBFC partnerships. Sources: [RBI Directions page](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Notification.aspx?Id=12848), [BillCut compliance blog](https://www.billcut.com/blogs/salary-advance-products-compliance-checklist). Quality: the RBI page was not read in full; the EWA statement is from a non-authoritative blog.
- **Gap:** **no source found on how employer-payroll-deduction EWA is treated**, or whether it counts as lending. This is the question the deck's "regulation of digital lending" risk turns on.
- **Competitors:** not searched.

### 3.6 RentRoute (rent by credit card)
- **The rule (press-reported):** the RBI updated its Payment Aggregator Master Directions on 15 Sep 2025, effective immediately; only merchants with a direct contract with the payment aggregator can receive card payments, so platforms can no longer route card payments to landlords who are not registered merchants. PhonePe, Paytm and CRED suspended credit-card rent payments within days; Amazon Pay, Freecharge and Mobikwik had paused earlier in 2024. HDFC Bank added a 1% charge on such transactions in June 2024; ICICI Bank and SBI Card stopped reward points on them. Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/phonepe-paytm-cred-end-rent-service-as-rbi-tightens-payment-rules-125091800422_1.html), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=532025), [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/cred-phonepe-paytm-stop-rent-payments-via-credit-cards-10474094), [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/rbis-new-rule-shakes-up-rent-payments-phonepe-paytm-to-shut-credit-card-option). Quality: press reports; **the RBI directions text was not read**. I found no Visa or Mastercard rule specific to rent.
- **Against the applicant's documents:**
  - RentRoute says it pays landlords' bank accounts through a payment-aggregator partner and has moved 6.1 Cr since July 2026, **after** the rule date. How the model fits the rule is unanswered in the files.
  - The deck's "no direct competitors" is contradicted by the period when large apps offered this service. Whether competitors still operate in some form was not researched.
  - The form acknowledges that banks and card networks "changed rules on rent payments before".
- **Not a finding:** I did not determine whether RentRoute complies. That needs the directions text, the aggregator agreement and the applicant's explanation.
