Version 1, 2026-11-24. Supersedes `thesis_v0.md` (kept as a working draft). Companion file: `fund_public_research.md` (sources, conflicts, pages not read).

This is a reconstruction, not fund policy. No scoring engine exists yet.

## 0. How to read this document

**Label (what kind of knowledge)**
- **DOCUMENTED** – stated in a source by the fund (its site, a partner's words in the notes, a memo's stated reason, or a press interview with a partner).
- **INFERRED** – derived by me from patterns across decisions. Not stated by the fund.
- **UNKNOWN** – no evidence in any file or source read.

**Confidence (how far to rely on it)**
- **High** – documented, and consistent with at least three decisions or two independent sources.
- **Medium** – documented once, or consistent with two to three decisions, or documented but dated or from a single outlet.
- **Low** – rests on one decision, conflicts between sources, or is a guess at a pattern.

**Evidence tags:** [B] case brief, [M1–M12] deal memos, [N1–N5] partner meeting notes, [P-site] allincapital.vc, [P-Inc42-23] Inc42 interview with Kushal Bhagia (9 Mar 2023), [P-Inc42-22] Inc42 fund announcement (9 Jun 2022), [P-Ent-25] Entrepreneur India Fund II launch (20 Mar 2025), [P-Ent-TorQ] Entrackr TorQLabs (12 Aug 2025). Full list in section 9.

**Cautions that apply throughout**
- Twelve memos is a small sample and every "why we decided" was written after the decision.
- The notes say they are "informal… not minutes and not a statement of policy" (header of the meeting notes).
- A pass is not a permanent rule: every pass memo except Coinlane and Prepnest states what would change the view (section 5).
- Kushal declined to write rules down because "founders learn to pass them" [N2]. The criteria below are things to weigh with evidence, not a checklist.

## 3. Notes on specific items

### 3.1 Stage
The brief says pre-seed; the site says pre-seed and seed. All six funded memos are "Early round" and all six passes are "Pre-seed". The public stage range explains the labels but does not show whether stage was a hidden criterion. UNKNOWN.

### 3.2 Written thesis vs observed behaviour [N5]
Riya says recent yeses would score lower against the public wording than several passes. Aditya says the pipeline changed, not the thesis. Kushal: both could be true. My reading of the portfolio page leans toward Riya on sector mix (INFERRED, Medium). This document does not settle it; the open question is which one scoring should use.

## 4. Documented vs inferred vs unknown (summary)
- **Documented, High:** India; pre-seed/seed; four focus sectors; founder-market fit; founder owns central risk; operator fluency for consumer brands; evidence over pedigree; conflict gate; regulation only with a plan; lending risk ownership; missing numbers drag scores.
- **Inferred, Medium or Low:** the B2B-software/commerce tilt; market size not a decider; crowding as a risk only; margin and revenue-proximity preferences; the generalised "ownership of the hard problem" pattern across all six passes.
- **Unknown:** ownership target; real cheque size; numeric traction thresholds; exclusions; weights; full portfolio; whether stage, healthcare or Series A are excluded; whether prior startup experience matters.
