## 2. How the fund describes its own thesis (public)
From the Inc42 interview with Bhagia (9 Mar 2023) and the official site:
- **Idea first:** what has changed in the world that makes this possible now (usually a technology or behaviour shift).
- **Founder-market fit:** what the founders know and can do; the key skills, the key risk, and "earned secrets" (insight from past experience).
- **First-time founders:** need proven ability to build (launched a product, ran something at scale, handled transactions).
- **Team shape:** likes long-time collaborators with complementary skills.
- **Grit:** a thesis and love for the problem; will not give up over 5-6 years.
- **Risk stance:** "no safe bets" at pre-seed; the fund describes its bets as contrarian and de-risks through price and volume, not safe picks. Entry valuation should be sensible so one winner can return the fund.
- **Avoids:** later-stage "safe" deals such as profitable companies, since it writes small cheques and would compete with large funds.
- **Site tagline:** "Going all in on founders who go all in."
- **Not discussed in the text read:** decks, scoring, ownership.

## 5. What this changes for the thesis
1. **Stage confound is mostly explained.** The fund publicly invests at pre-seed and seed. All six invested memos being "Early round" fits that; the brief's "pre-seed" is a simplification. Stage still cannot be dropped as a criterion, but it is no longer a mystery.
2. **Portfolio mix supports Riya's drift.** The official list is dominated by B2B software and commerce infrastructure (Rocketlane, Mailmodo, MeetRecord, Spiti.xyz, Suitable.ai, RevenueHero, Bitespeed, FleetX, Fashinza, SuperK, Anar), not by the four sectors in the brief. Public sectors and revealed behaviour do diverge.
3. **Web3 is not excluded outright.** The portfolio includes QuestBook and Spiti.xyz. The Coinlane pass was about dependence on favourable regulation for a retail crypto exchange, so the gate should be worded around that, not around "Web3".
4. **Cheque size is only partly resolved.** A public range exists but conflicts across sources and time. The fund's real cheque for a given company is still unknown.
5. **Conflict checks now have a public list**, with the gap that it is incomplete.

## 6. Portfolio overlap flags for the 10 applications (for review, not conclusions)
- **Talkbook vs MeetRecord:** Talkbook sells AI call summaries and CRM updates for sales teams; MeetRecord is a portfolio company in the same broad area per Meeting 4. The MeetRecord product description has not yet been read, so the degree of overlap is unconfirmed. Treat as a likely conflict flag.
- **StockSathi vs SuperK:** both serve small-town kirana stores (restocking and credit vs rebranding and supply). Adjacent; degree unknown. StockSathi says it was introduced by a Fund I portfolio founder, which the system should note.
- **Possible category neighbours** (weaker): EarnWise and Settlr in fintech lending alongside Wint Wealth and Jai Kisan; CAPilot, Talkbook, Pumpwise next to the B2B software and logistics names. Needs product-level comparison.
